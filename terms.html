Gemelo — Terms of Use

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Not a safety tool. Gemelo gives an AI estimate from photos and shows look-alikes. It never says a mushroom can be eaten. Never eat a wild mushroom based on an app — show it to a local expert. If someone may have eaten a wild mushroom, call poison control or emergency services now.

What Gemelo is

An educational field guide and photo-based look-alike checker. Results are estimates that can be wrong. Gemelo is not a substitute for an in-hand identification by a qualified expert and must never be used to decide whether to eat a mushroom. You use it at your own risk.
