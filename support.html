Support

Questions, a wrong result, or something broken: netkaktakto@gmail.com. For a wrong identification use "Report wrong ID" in the result; it prepares an email with the shortlist.

QThe check says "not sure"

That is intended. Add the angle the app asks for (usually the base dug out whole, or the underside of the cap) and try again.

QThe check does not start or times out

The check needs an internet connection. Try again on a stronger signal; a busy server answers with a request to retry shortly.

QRestore my purchase

Open the paywall or Settings and tap Restore, signed in with the Apple ID you subscribed with.

QCancel

iOS Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Gemelo.

QPoison-control numbers

Settings › Your ground sets the region; the hotline button is then one tap away. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

Gemelo never says a mushroom is edible. Ask a local expert.
