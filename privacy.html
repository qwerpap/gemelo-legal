Gemelo — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Not a safety tool. Gemelo gives an AI estimate from photos and shows look-alikes. It never says a mushroom can be eaten. Never eat a wild mushroom based on an app — show it to a local expert. If someone may have eaten a wild mushroom, call poison control or emergency services now.

Gemelo checks a mushroom against its dangerous look-alikes. We built it to keep your data on your iPhone.

What stays on your device

Your quiz answers, field notes, places, notes and saved photos are stored only on your iPhone. There is no account and no server copy of your notes.

Photos you choose to check

When you run a check, and only after you agree in the app, the one to four photos you took are sent through our own server to OpenAI's vision service to shortlist species from Gemelo's built-in catalogue. They are used only to answer that request. Our server does not store them. OpenAI processes them under its API data policy (API inputs are not used to train its models). You can turn this off any time in Settings.

Purchases and analytics

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. We use Adapty to confirm your subscription and to count onboarding steps, paywall views and purchases; it receives an anonymous app profile ID, your quiz answers (where you look, region, reason) and purchase events — not your name, photos or notes. We don't use advertising or third-party analytics SDKs, and we don't collect IDFA.

Reports

"Report wrong ID" opens your own mail app with the shortlist; nothing is sent unless you send that email.

Children

Gemelo is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.

Deleting data

Delete any note in the app, or delete the app to remove everything.
