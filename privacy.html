Privacy policy

Gemelo helps you compare a wild mushroom with its toxic look-alikes. Most of what you do stays on your iPhone. One feature, the photo check, needs a server, and this page says exactly what happens to those photos.

01Stays on your iPhone

Field notes, saved finds with their photos, places, your region, your quiz answers and settings are stored in the app on your phone. There is no account and no copy of your notes on our side. Photos you take for a find are saved as files next to the app's data.

02The photo check

What is sent
One to four photos you chose for a check, with the angle label of each (cap, underside, base, cut). Nothing else from your library. The app asks before the first check.
Where to
Our own proxy, a Firebase Cloud Function in Google Cloud (us-central1), which forwards the photos to an AI vision model. The model is Google's Gemini, run through Replicate; if it fails, OpenAI's vision model is used as a backup.
Why
To shortlist the species from Gemelo's built-in catalogue that your mushroom could be, with their dangerous look-alikes.
Kept?
Our proxy does not save your photos or the answer. The AI providers process them to return the result and handle them under their own terms.

If you do not want photos to leave your phone, do not run a check; the look-alike tables and field notes work without it. Choosing a photo from your gallery needs no photo-library permission, and the camera asks for permission only when you take a picture.

03Camera

Gemelo asks for camera access to photograph a mushroom from several angles. Pictures are used only for the check you start and for the find you save.

04Purchases and measurement

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We use Adapty to confirm that Gemelo Pro is active and to count onboarding steps, paywall views and purchases. Adapty receives a random profile ID, your onboarding answers (where you look, your region, why you forage) and purchase events. It never receives your photos, notes, name or email. There are no ad SDKs and no tracking across other apps or sites.

05Reports and phone calls

"Report wrong ID" opens your mail app with the shortlist filled in; it is sent only if you press Send. The poison-control button hands a phone number to the Phone app; the call is made by you, not by Gemelo.

06Deleting

Delete any find or note inside the app, or delete Gemelo to remove everything stored on the phone. To ask about the anonymous subscription profile, write to the address below.

Safety. Gemelo never says a mushroom is safe to eat and is not a substitute for an expert. If someone has eaten a wild mushroom, call poison control or emergency services at once.

07Children, changes, contact

Gemelo is not directed at children. Changes to this policy appear here with a new date. Contact: netkaktakto@gmail.com.
